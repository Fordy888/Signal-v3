# AI Adoption Proof — Verified Source Notes

## McKinsey — The state of AI in 2026: On the road to ROI

URL: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai

Published: 25 August 2026. Survey of 1,719 participants in 97 countries, fielded 4 May–8 June 2026.

Verified findings: 44% report AI scaling across the enterprise, up from 38%; 80% report improved individual productivity; 50% report better decisions; 37% attribute at least some EBIT impact to AI; 32% decided against buying software because agentic coding tools could build it internally; 20% say AI operating costs constrained use; 14% report AI contributed to an overall workforce decline in the past year. High performers fundamentally redesign workflows rather than insert AI into existing work.

## Reuters — Strong majority of Japanese firms have yet to fully embrace AI

URL: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/strong-majority-japanese-firms-have-yet-fully-embrace-ai-2026-08-12/

Published: 12 August 2026. Reuters/Nikkei Research survey; 219 of 510 companies responded.

Verified findings: more than 80% use AI only in limited operations or not at all; 16% have deployed AI company-wide as an integral tool; 60% use it only in parts of the company; 18% have not decided whether to introduce AI; 6% are not considering it. One wholesaler reported company-wide use limited to document creation. AI budget expectations included 4% expecting 50%+ annual growth and 21% expecting 10%–50% growth.

## Reuters — Europe’s established tech firms emerge as unexpected AI winners

URL: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/europes-established-tech-firms-emerge-unexpected-ai-winners-2026-08-05/

Published: 5 August 2026.

Verified findings: SAP cloud backlog rose 26% at constant currencies to €22.9 billion as companies moved finance, procurement, supply-chain and HR systems toward AI-ready platforms; Capgemini bookings rose 9.2%; Sopra Steria organic growth accelerated to 5.3%; OVHcloud public-cloud revenue rose 20.2%. Airbus plans about 70 critical applications on Scaleway by end-2028. The practical theme is model integration with legacy systems, data, permissions, audit trails and workflows.

## Thomson Reuters Institute — Turning law firm AI strategies into practice

URL: https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/institute/reports/turning-law-firm-ai-strategies-into-practice

Published: 6 August 2026. Based on 116 interviews with law-firm leaders/managing partners and 2,527 interviews with client-identified stand-out lawyers.

Verified findings: nearly 80% believe their practice has a clear AI integration plan, but fewer than half are confident in their practice area’s ability to succeed as AI becomes integrated. Embedded daily AI users were nine times more likely than minimal/non-users to report significant efficiency and quality impact. Only 25% strongly agreed their firm had a plan to monetise AI use. More than one-third had discussed AI with fewer than 20% of clients. The report recommends moving from hours-saved metrics to turnaround, risk identification, consistency, satisfaction and capacity measures.

## Marketing Dive — IAB revisits AI disclosure in ads

URL: https://www.marketingdive.com/news/iab-revisits-ai-disclosure-in-ads-as-legal-requirements-multiply/828182/

Published: August 2026. Reports the IAB’s second AI Transparency and Disclosure Framework.

Verified findings: 83% of advertising executives reported using AI in the creative process, up 23 percentage points from 2024; 72% of marketers believe the industry should have a disclosure standard; and 73% of millennial and Gen Z consumers said an AI-generated ad would have no effect on, or increase, purchase likelihood. The practical consequence is a new operating decision for creative production, disclosure and brand trust as labelling rules take effect across three continents.

## CX Dive — AI adoption in CX is accelerating, but few projects produce value

URL: https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/ai-adoption-in-cx-is-accelerating-but-few-projects-produce-value/827869/

Published: August 2026. Based on a survey of more than 600 customer-experience leaders and practitioners.

Verified findings: AI adoption in CX rose from 57% to 70%, but only 2% of adopting organisations were reported as seeing actual value in improved outcomes and ROI. Fifty-four percent use purpose-built CX AI operating models; about one-quarter use help-desk add-ons; and 22% built in-house. A separate cited survey found three-quarters had rolled back a customer-facing AI agent after deployment. The practical lesson is to start with a measurable customer workflow and known friction rather than a top-down tool mandate.

## Deutsche Bank — Financial Research Agent

URL: https://www.db.com/news/detail/20260825-deutsche-bank-helps-shape-google-cloud-s-new-ai-solution-for-financial-services?language_id=1

Published: 25 August 2026.

Verified findings: Deutsche Bank co-designed Google Cloud’s Financial Research Agent for regulated banking workflows and will use it in Corporate Bank teams serving German MidCorp clients. The agent combines company, market and financial information into structured, traceable outputs intended to reduce manual research, improve consistency and auditability, and free time for client conversations. Controls include permissions, encryption, audit logging, access management and data residency. No quantified realised outcome was claimed, so this source is suitable for Newsroom, not Focus.

## Salesforce — 2026 Agentic Enterprise Index

URL: https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentic-enterprise-index-insights-2026/

Published: 7 August 2026. Vendor-owned analysis of aggregated Agentforce usage among businesses with production agents every month from February 2025 to April 2026.

Verified findings: average activated agents per organisation nearly tripled and creation time fell 53%; agent work output grew at a 15% compound monthly rate; retailers using AI agents recorded 8% year-on-year sales growth versus 2% without agents. Pandora’s AI concierge handled 60% of routine support requests and lifted Net Promoter Score 10%. Salesforce reports its internal Slackbot is used regularly by 83% of staff and saves the average employee up to five hours per week. The source is empirical but vendor-owned, so claims must be attributed and balanced rather than treated as independent proof.

## Gartner — Customer-service AI spending

URL: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-08-26-gartner-survey-finds-ai-spending-by-customer-service-leaders-has-surged-by-38-percent-despite-overall-service-and-support-function-budgets-rising-by-just-2-percent

Published: 26 August 2026. Survey of 199 service and support leaders conducted in April–May 2026.

Verified findings: AI spending increased 38% while total customer-service and support budgets grew 2%. Leaders are redirecting spending from labour and overhead toward technology, but Gartner cautions that the investment must be tied to measurable business value. Respondents expect generative-AI chatbots, voicebots and agentic platforms to become the most valuable service technologies over the next two years.

## CX Dive — Only one-quarter of AI customer-service use cases produce ROI

URL: https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/only-one-quarter-of-ai-customer-service-use-cases-produce-roi/827951/

Published: August 2026. Reports Gartner analysis of 432 customer-service AI use cases.

Verified findings: 25% produced a positive return, 25% produced negative returns, 42% had unclear ROI and 11% broke even. Service teams pursue nearly five AI use cases on average and commit about 13% of functional budgets to AI; 56% of service leaders expect incentives tied directly to AI outcomes in 2026. The report distinguishes customer resolution from superficial containment.

## Art of Procurement — State of AI in Procurement 2026

URL: https://artofprocurement.com/blog/state-of-ai-in-procurement

Updated: 20 August 2026. Synthesis article with linked underlying research.

Verified findings: 94% of procurement executives use generative AI weekly, but only 4% of teams had reached large-scale deployment after 49% ran pilots. Reported use cases include spend analytics, RFP/RFQ generation, contract summarisation, supplier risk and negotiation. Underlying research cited in the article places average investments at $1.0 million for forecasting/budgeting use cases, $2.6 million for supplier management and $2.0 million for supplier-risk monitoring. The practical point is the gap between individual use and embedded production workflows.

FOUNDER'S NOTE

AI earns attention when it changes the business.

AI is no longer interesting because it can do something clever. It earns attention when it changes revenue, margin, work, customers or risk. That is the line we hold in Signal. Six items show AI changing business; four show the market changing around it. Read both. Judge what moves your business. — Paul

WHY IT MATTERS

AI is spreading faster than its returns

AI use is expanding across software, logistics, sales and work, but financial proof remains uneven. At the same time, oil, rates, trade and legacy costs are reshaping the conditions in which leaders invest. The opportunity is real; operating discipline decides who captures it.

WHAT TO DO NOW

ACT NOW

Choose one commercial outcome

Tie one AI initiative to revenue, margin, cash, customers or risk before funding another tool.

WATCH CLOSELY

Redesign the whole workflow

Check whether AI removes the bottleneck or merely moves it to the next manual step.

OPPORTUNITY

Keep market pressure visible

Test AI investment against higher costs, rates and supply uncertainty—not against a frictionless plan.

THE OTHER SIDE

Adoption still outruns the evidence

These signals show real AI adoption and some commercial traction, but they do not prove broad enterprise returns. Different sectors, workflows and labour markets are moving at different speeds.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR VIEW

Several quarters showed wider profit, productivity and customer gains from AI across industries rather than mainly among technology suppliers and isolated high performers.

WATCH FOR THIS

Watch whether AI reaches the profit line

• Whether AI productivity gains begin producing broader enterprise earnings and customer improvements.

• Whether logistics and other operators connect isolated tools into complete workflows.

• Whether retraining remains more common than job reduction as workplace adoption spreads.

FOUNDER'S NOTE

Stop counting AI tools. Count changed work.

The AI stories worth your time are not about who launched the cleverest model. They are about what changed inside the business: a workflow moved faster, a customer got a better answer, a cost came out or revenue went up. Today’s evidence carries a warning too. Adoption is easy to announce and hard to convert into value. Start with the work, name the measure and let the result earn the next investment. — Paul

WHY IT MATTERS

AI adoption is outrunning operating proof

The pattern is clear: AI has moved into real work across operations, legal services, customer experience, procurement, advertising and retail. The value gap is just as clear. Leaders are funding tools faster than they are redesigning workflows, pricing the outcome or measuring the return.

WHAT TO DO NOW

ACT NOW

Name the workflow before the tool

Choose one customer or operating process and define what better means before adding AI.

WATCH CLOSELY

Put value beside adoption

Track revenue, cost, speed, quality or risk—not licences, prompts or demonstrations.

OPPORTUNITY

Price the changed work

If AI improves delivery, decide how customers, margins and staff should share the gain.

THE OTHER SIDE

Adoption data is not the same as causation

Several figures come from surveys or vendor platforms. They show where AI is being used and where results are claimed, but they do not prove every gain came from AI alone.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR VIEW

Independent, audited results showed sustained margin, revenue and customer gains across multiple quarters and business sizes.

WATCH FOR THIS

Watch the distance between use and value

• Whether enterprise earnings begin catching up with reported employee productivity gains.

• Whether customer-service leaders cut weak AI projects rather than funding adoption for appearance.

• Whether professional firms convert faster work into clearer client value and smarter pricing.

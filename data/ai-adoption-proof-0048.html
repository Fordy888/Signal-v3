FOUNDER'S NOTE

AI adoption does not pause for the weekend. Neither should your judgement.

The useful stories today are about operating choices, not model theatre. A council is moving planning applications faster. A bank is putting account enquiries inside Claude. Software buyers are changing how they build a shortlist. The pattern is simple: AI creates value when it changes real work, but every gain creates a new dependency—on data, suppliers, pricing or governance. Ask what moved, what it cost and what happens if the provider changes the rules. — Paul

WHY IT MATTERS

AI is moving into the workflow—and taking dependencies with it

Today’s adoption evidence is practical: planning decisions, banking enquiries, loan servicing, freight routes, software buying, customer experience and work management. The gains are real enough to act on. So are the dependencies. Value now rests on permissions, data quality, pricing design, implementation skill and supplier continuity—not access to a model alone.

WHAT TO DO NOW

ACT NOW

Map the dependency beside the gain

For every AI workflow, name the data, permission, supplier and person it cannot operate without.

WATCH CLOSELY

Faster buying still needs easier selling

If customers use AI to shortlist you, remove quote, approval and implementation friction before demand reaches the hand-off.

OPPORTUNITY

Borrow the council test

Pick one service process and compare its speed with a credible external benchmark, not with your own activity count.

THE OTHER SIDE

A number can be real and still need context

Several figures come from company statements, vendor platforms or an early working paper. They identify meaningful operating shifts, but they do not prove that every claimed gain came from AI alone.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR VIEW

Independent results showed that the same workflow gains disappeared after controlling for staffing, process redesign and customer mix.

WATCH FOR THIS

Watch what sits behind the headline number

• Whether AI-enabled service improvements hold when demand, exceptions and complaints rise.

• Whether software sellers redesign the purchase path as quickly as buyers redesign research.

• Whether major AI-platform deals preserve genuine model choice after the announcement.

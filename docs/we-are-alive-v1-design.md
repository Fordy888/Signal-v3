# DTL Signal — CEO View + WE ARE ALIVE Design V1

## Governing experience

The enhanced daily experience follows **Think → Decide → Look up → Smile**.

| Rhythm | Signal component |
|---|---|
| Think | THE ONE THING, THE EVIDENCE and optional Visual Signal |
| Decide | Interpretation, **CEO View**, WHAT CHANGED and Executive Action |
| Look up | Counter-Signal, What to Watch and, when eligible, WE ARE ALIVE |
| Smile | Dad Joke of the Day |

CEO View remains the named human judgement layer. It must not repeat the evidence or masquerade as source fact.

## WE ARE ALIVE publication rule

**Every daily edition is eligible; no edition is entitled to the feature.**

The moment appears only when all four conditions are satisfied:

1. The natural phenomenon is current or genuinely seasonally current in the stated place.
2. The photograph is real, high quality and depicts the same place/phenomenon described by the caption.
3. Commercial reproduction rights are explicit and the required attribution is retained.
4. Recent location, category and species checks show sufficient novelty.

If any condition is uncertain, the edition proceeds without WE ARE ALIVE.

## Hard exclusions

No AI-generated image, uncertain licence, scraped news image, natural disaster, distressing wildlife, death, catastrophe framing, campaign imagery, tourism promotion, influencer staging, sponsored content, inspirational quote, business metaphor, CTA, engagement question or click optimisation is permitted.

## Proof candidate

The initial proof uses a real photograph of a humpback whale and calf taken off **Moorea, French Polynesia** by **Charles J. Sharp**. Wikimedia Commons records the photograph as the photographer’s own work under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Tahiti Tourisme records the official humpback whale-watching season as **20 July to 20 November**, making 24 August seasonally current. The caption does not imply that the photograph was taken on the edition date.

## Human control

Discovery may propose a candidate, but governed validation determines eligibility. The initial implementation remains feature-flagged and requires a human-approved candidate object before rendering. Publication history is recorded only after successful delivery.
